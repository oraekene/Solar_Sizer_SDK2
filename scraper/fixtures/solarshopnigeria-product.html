The JKM535M-72HL4-TV is a 535 Wp bifacial photovoltaic module from JinkoSolar's Eagle 72HM G5b series, built with 144 (2x72) P-type mono-crystalline half-cut cells and a transparent backsheet that lets the rear side add extra yield. It is intended for grid-tied residential, commercial and utility-scale arrays on 1500 V systems. Key strengths are 20.75% front-side efficiency, a bifacial factor of 70±5%, Fire Type 1 rating with 3.2 mm tempered front glass, and a 30-year linear performance warranty.
