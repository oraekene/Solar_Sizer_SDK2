The Jinko Solar JKS-B51100-II is a 5.12kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery module with a nominal voltage of 51.2V, designed for low-voltage residential solar storage and backup systems. It uses plug-and-play connectors and can be stacked and paralleled up to 16 units for larger capacity. RS485 and CAN communication allow it to work with compatible hybrid inverters, and it is rated for 5000 cycles with a 5-year warranty.
